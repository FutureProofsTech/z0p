\begin{abstract}
We present \textbf{z0p}, a complete zero-knowledge proving stack implemented
from first principles in safe Rust with no dependencies beyond the compiler
and the operating system. Binary fields, the BabyBear prime field, BLAKE3,
Merkle trees, a parallel NTT, an arity-4 FRI polynomial commitment, four
STARK systems (Fibonacci AIR, range checks, table membership, and hash-folded
IVC), LogUp lookups, zero-knowledge masking, and a compact multilinear PCS
prototype are all owned by a single crate under
\texttt{\#![forbid(unsafe\_code)]}. The system is
transparent (no trusted setup) and hash-based, hence plausibly
post-quantum. Production parameters target a $\approx 100$-bit
query/proof-of-work schedule (rate $1/16$, 40 queries, 20-bit grinding).
FRI folds, STARK composition batches, \texttt{upcs}/Gemini points and
betas, sumcheck rounds, and lookup identities draw challenges from a
quartic extension $\mathbb{F}_{p^4}$, raising per-challenge algebraic
bounds to $\approx 124$ bits (see Section~\ref{sec:security}). Measured on a
16-core machine, a 1,048,576-step computation proves in 5.19~s
($\approx 4.95\,\mu$s/step), verifies in 2.3~ms, with a 206,353-byte proof.
Validation combines 252 unit tests with three adversarial suites
($\approx 800$ hostile attempts per run, zero acceptances, zero panics),
wire-exact size assertions on every benchmark, and machine-checked
regression gates.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Practical zero-knowledge infrastructure asks its users to trust a great
deal: pairing-friendly elliptic curves, multi-party setup ceremonies,
deep dependency trees, and provers whose soundness stories live in slide
decks. At the same time, large-scale quantum computing threatens every
discrete-logarithm and pairing-based proof system in deployment. The
systems that survive that transition will be hash-based, transparently
set up, small enough to audit, and tested hard enough to believe.

\textbf{Contributions.} z0p is, to our knowledge, the first \emph{complete}
STARK stack --- fields, hash, Merkle trees, NTT, polynomial commitment,
AIR systems, and zero-knowledge masking --- implemented from scratch in
safe Rust with zero dependencies:
\begin{itemize}
\item A transparent, plausibly post-quantum design: BLAKE3-256 digests and
FRI Reed--Solomon commitments over BabyBear. No curves, no ceremonies.
\item Production parameter sizing in one call
(\texttt{Params::for\_security(\dots, 100)}): blowup 16, queries,
grinding, final layer, and cap height jointly optimized for a
$\approx 100$-bit query/PoW schedule. All algebraic challenges use a
quartic extension field.
\item Competitive scalar performance with parallelism over
\texttt{std::thread} only: 1M-step proofs at $\approx 202$k steps/s,
millisecond verification, logarithmically growing proofs.
\item Adversarial validation as a first-class artifact: every forgery
class we could construct is tested continuously, including a fuzzer that
once caught a genuine proof-malleability hole (since closed by binding
dimensions into the transcript).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Related work}

STARKs \cite{BBHR18a} and the FRI proximity test \cite{BBHR18b},
strengthened by DEEP-FRI \cite{BBHR19} and compiled via
BCS-style Fiat--Shamir \cite{BCS16}, are our direct lineage. Production
STARK toolchains (notably Plonky2/Polygon Zero \cite{Pol22} and Risc
Zero) share the BabyBear-plus-FRI engineering core but retain large
dependency footprints. We differ in taking dependency-freedom and
auditable minimalism as hard constraints, and in publishing adversarial
test evidence alongside performance claims. Our lookups follow the
LogUp logarithmic-derivative design \cite{Hab22}. The hash is
BLAKE3 \cite{BLAKE3}; Fiat--Shamir is classical \cite{FS86}.

\section{Preliminaries}

\paragraph{Fields.} Program words use binary fields $GF(2^8)$,
$GF(2^{64})$, $GF(2^{128})$. Polynomial domains use BabyBear
$\mathbb{F}_p$, $p = 2^{31}-2^{27}+1 = 2013265921$ with generator
$31$; $p-1 = 2^{27}\cdot 15$, so $2^k$-roots of unity exist for
$k \le 27$. Elements serialize as 4 canonical little-endian bytes;
multiplication is Barrett reduction, inversion Fermat
($0 \mapsto 0$), batch inversion Montgomery's trick. FRI values and fold
challenges live in the quartic tower
$\mathbb{F}_{p^2}=\mathbb{F}_p[u]/(u^2-11)$,
$\mathbb{F}_{p^4}=\mathbb{F}_{p^2}[v]/(v^2-u)$; $11$ is a quadratic
non-residue and $u$ a non-square; extension inversion uses the tower norm
$ (c+dv)^{-1}=(c-dv)/(c^2-ud^2) $. Elements serialize as four canonical
coordinates (16 bytes).

\paragraph{Domains.} Trace domain
$H = \{1,\omega,\dots,\omega^{N-1}\}$; evaluation domain
$D = \gamma\cdot\langle\Omega\rangle$ of size $M = N\cdot\beta$
($\beta$ the blowup), coset shift $\gamma = 31$; $M \le 2^{27}$
enforced everywhere. Vanishing polynomial $Z_H(x) = x^N - 1$.

\paragraph{Hash and Merkle trees.} Unkeyed BLAKE3-256, implemented from
the specification and validated against reference vectors including
chunk boundaries. Trees use
$\mathrm{leaf}(d) = H(0x00\|d)$,
$\mathrm{node}(l,r) = H(0x01\|l\|r)$ (second-preimage resistant).
\emph{Caps} expose an upper tree layer in the commitment so openings
carry only middle path segments: column trees drop
$\min(h,\mathrm{depth})$ levels, FRI quad layers
$\min(h,\mathrm{depth}-2)$ (the verifier rebuilds the bottom two
levels from revealed values).

\paragraph{Transcripts.} Append-only, length-prefix-framed Fiat--Shamir
transcript with a full domain-separation schedule (FRI:
\texttt{fri-dims} first, then per-digest \texttt{fri-layer-root}, three
\texttt{fri-fold} challenges per layer, \texttt{fri-final},
\texttt{fri-pow}, and per-query \texttt{fri-query}; per-protocol
\texttt{params}, \texttt{column}, and \texttt{alpha} labels; IVC
\texttt{fold/step}, \texttt{fold/trace}, six \texttt{fold/alpha} per
step plus per-step \texttt{fold/gamma}; lookup and sumcheck labels
analogously). Proof-of-work grinding finds the smallest nonce with $d$
leading zero bits under the history commitment; difficulties above 256
always fail.

\section{FRI polynomial commitment}

\texttt{commit} encodes $n$ coefficients to $M = n\beta$ coset
evaluations (shifted NTT); \texttt{commit\_evals} takes
caller-supplied LDE values of declared degree $\le \mathrm{bound} < M$.
Both enforce the rate gate $\mathrm{bound}+1 \le M/2$ at prove
\emph{and} verify time. Prover-echoed dimensions are absorbed under
\texttt{fri-dims} before any challenge, closing proof malleability of
those bytes.

Layers are bit-reversed; each adjacent quad sits at
$(x,-x,j_0x,-j_0x)$ for the fixed fourth root $j_0$, sharing $x^4$.
Writing $P(X) = A+BX+CX^2+DX^3$ in $Y = X^4$, each layer folds as
$A + r_0B + r_1C + r_2D$ with fresh challenges
$r_i \in \mathbb{F}_{p^4}$ (values embed base-field elements in the first
coordinate), quartering the degree; point inverses are batch-inverted
once per layer and folding is multi-threaded. Proofs carry the final layer in the clear plus, per
query, all four values of every folded quad with middle path segments.
The verifier replays the transcript, checks dimensions, rate, cap
shapes, and PoW, verifies the final layer by divided differences
against residual bound $\mathrm{bound} \gg 2f$, then checks every
Merkle path and fold relation, chaining intermediate parents forward.

\begin{conjecture}[FRI soundness sizing]
Under the Johnson-radius heuristic ($\delta \approx 1-\sqrt{\rho}$),
rate $\rho = 1/8$ with 12 queries gives $\approx 18$ bits (bench
shape); rate $\rho = 1/16$ with 40 queries plus 20-bit grinding gives
an $80 + 20 = 100$-bit query/PoW schedule (production shape). Caps affect
size only. These counts exclude the finite-field algebraic term. FRI
folds, the AIR/range/table/fold composition batches, \texttt{upcs}/Gemini
evaluation points and fold betas, sumcheck rounds, and lookup's
rational-identity challenge all draw $\mathbb{F}_{p^4}$ challenges, so a
nonzero degree-$d$ identity vanishes with probability about $d/p^4$ per
challenge. The former approximately 31-bit algebraic ceiling is gone.
The production schedule remains a query/PoW formula, not an end-to-end
soundness proof.
\end{conjecture}

\section{AIR systems}

All application protocols share one pattern: interpolate columns,
extend on the LDE coset, Merkle-commit joint rows, evaluate a
composition vector (geometric vanishing recurrence with
Montgomery-batched denominators, bit-identical to scalar evaluation),
FRI-commit it, open trace columns at FRI query positions, and
re-verify the identity per query.

\paragraph{Fibonacci AIR.} Trace $A[0]=a_0$, $B[0]=b_0$,
$A[i+1]=B[i]$, $B[i+1]=A[i]+B[i]$, claim $B[N-1]=b_{\mathrm{last}}$.
With $w_{\mathrm{last}}=\omega^{N-1}$ and
$Z_{\mathrm{trans}}(x)=(x^N-1)/(x-w_{\mathrm{last}})$:
\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
C(x) ={}& \alpha_0\frac{a'-b}{Z_{\mathrm{trans}}(x)}
+ \alpha_1\frac{b'-(a+b)}{Z_{\mathrm{trans}}(x)}
+ \alpha_2\frac{a-a_0}{x-1} \\
&+ \alpha_3\frac{b-b_0}{x-1}
+ \alpha_4\frac{b-b_{\mathrm{last}}}{x-w_{\mathrm{last}}}.
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
Dividing (not multiplying) by $Z_{\mathrm{trans}}$ is load-bearing: the
quotient is low-degree iff the transition holds on every covered row.

\paragraph{Range checks.} Value column plus $n\le 24$ bit columns:
decomposition $(V-\sum 2^jC_j)/Z_H$, per-bit boolean quotients
$C_j(C_j-1)/Z_H$, and a boundary quotient.

\paragraph{Table membership.}
$C(x)=\alpha_{\mathrm{member}}\prod_{t\in T}(v-t)/Z_H(x)
+ \alpha_{\mathrm{bound}}(v-\mathrm{value})/(x-1)$;
small tables by design.

\paragraph{Folding (IVC).} Chained segments share one batched FRI
commitment over $\sum_s \gamma_s C_s$ with per-step challenges;
one proof covers the chain.

\paragraph{Lookups.} LogUp-style: multiplicities, $\beta$ challenge,
quotients $1/(\beta+w)$, $m/(\beta+t)$, rational-sum equality and four
sumchecks; proving fails closed on out-of-table witnesses. A committed
variant (\texttt{lookup\_pcs}) commits the witness, table,
multiplicities, and both quotient columns with BaseFold, binds every
sumcheck final value with a point opening, and folds the total
multiplicity weight into the sum-t claim with a fresh $\gamma$. At
1,024-in-1,024 with cap height 4 it proves in 367\,ms, verifies in
21\,ms, and carries 642,443 bytes. It is committed-input but not
zero-knowledge, does not constrain columns to the base field, and pays
for eight separate openings.

\paragraph{Zero knowledge.} Masked proving blinds columns with
vanishing multiples from a ChaCha20 stream
($n_{\mathrm{rand}}\ge$ queries enforced; key material zeroized on
drop). Openings become witness-independent; verification is unchanged,
and masked false statements still fail.

\section{Encodings and parameters}

Wire format (\texttt{VERSION}~=~4): counts as $u32$, dimensions as
$u64$, base fields as 4 canonical bytes and extension values as 16
canonical bytes ($<p$ per coordinate), digests as 32 bytes, direction
bits $0/1$. Lookup and sumcheck proofs use the same framing, with
canonical extension values and nested versioned sumcheck bodies. The decoder fails closed on wrong
versions, trailing bytes, truncation, non-canonical fields, and counts
past hard caps ($2^{20}$ queries, $64$ folds, $128$ path steps,
$256$ cap digests, $2^{27}$ elements, $2^{20}$ IVC steps) before any
allocation. Validation: power-of-two domains, blowup $\ge 4$ for AIR,
$M/\mathrm{final}$ a power of four, $M \le 2^{27}$.

\section{Security analysis}\label{sec:security}

\paragraph{Assumptions.} BLAKE3 collision/preimage resistance; FRI
soundness heuristic; Fiat--Shamir in the (Q)ROM. No discrete-log,
pairing, or setup assumptions: Shor-inert by construction.

\paragraph{Enforced invariants.} Bilateral rate gate;
verifier-derived domains and query indices; final divided-difference
check at true folded points; cap/path shape pins before hashing;
\texttt{fri-dims} dimension binding; canonical-only decoding; PoW
bound to history with queries sampled after.

\paragraph{Adversarial validation.} Three layers: 252 unit tests
(KATs, Horner cross-checks, parallel-vs-sequential identity); a
13-vector security suite (tampering of every query/fold/value/path/
digest, 300 single-bit-flip fuzz trials, PoW forgery, rate gates both
directions, statement replay); and a 10-vector red-team suite
(cross-proof transplants, false statements, IVC surgery, codec-limit
abuse, transcript abuse, exact rate boundaries, masked soundness, and
a 500-input garbage storm that must fail closed without panicking).
Standing: $\approx 800$ hostile attempts per run, zero acceptances,
zero panics, in debug and release.

\section{Implementation and performance}

Safe Rust, zero dependencies, parallelism over \texttt{std::thread}
only (NTT stages $\ge 2^{16}$, folding, Merkle levels; all
bit-identical). Key optimizations: Barrett field arithmetic,
table-driven NTT, single-chunk hash fast paths, batched composition
(geometric vanishing $+$ Montgomery denominators), verifier on-demand
domain points, Merkle caps. Measured 2026-10-20, release, medians,
16-core/31\,GB, \texttt{rustc} 1.99.0:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrr}
\toprule
Workload (production query/PoW schedule) & Prove & Verify & Size \\
\midrule
AIR 1,024 steps & 39\,ms & 1.2\,ms & 71,373\,B \\
AIR 8,192 steps & 81\,ms & 1.6\,ms & 104,033\,B \\
AIR 65,536 steps & 332\,ms & 2.1\,ms & 144,441\,B \\
AIR \textbf{1,048,576} steps & \textbf{5.19\,s} & \textbf{2.3\,ms} & \textbf{206,353\,B} \\
FRI poly-1024 & 71\,ms & 0.95\,ms & 43,174\,B \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

Marginal cost $\approx 4.95\,\mu$s/step ($\approx 202$k steps/s);
proof sizes are deterministic and checked against the wire format every run.
Verification is size-independent: each FRI domain point is derived on
demand in $O(\log)$ field operations rather than from a materialized
$O(\mathrm{lde})$ layer table, so verify stays in the low milliseconds
from $1{,}024$ to $1{,}048{,}576$ steps. Primitives: BLAKE3 1\,MiB in
1.42\,ms, BabyBear $\approx 136$\,Mops/s, NTT $2^{14}$ in 0.33\,ms,
64k-leaf Merkle tree in 3.96\,ms.

\paragraph{Compact multilinear PCS prototype.} On 1,024 coefficients with
blowup 16, 40 queries, and 20-bit grinding, the BaseFold-style prototype
proves in 44\,ms, verifies in 1.1\,ms, and produces a 127,658-byte proof;
cap height 4 gives 45\,ms / 0.9\,ms and 80,018 bytes. The Gemini v1
construction on the same shape takes 693\,ms / 54\,ms and 888,672 bytes.
The prototype commits one root, reduces $f(r)$ to $c(\rho)$ with
sumcheck, and uses Möbius FRI folds. Optional Merkle caps store a digest
frontier per layer and hash it back to the layer root; decoder fuzzing
covers both plain and capped proofs. It backs the committed lookup
prototype but is not wired into the AIR stack and has no end-to-end
soundness proof; treat these numbers as an engineering result, not an
audit.

\section{Limitations and future work}

Bench defaults are fast but weak and must not be mistaken for
production soundness. The table AIR is small-table by design; the
committed LogUp prototype removes clear witness/table inputs but still
uses eight separate BaseFold openings, lacks hiding, and does not enforce
base-field column values. Vectorized hashing and the one-query recursion
fragment are complete, FRI-backed univariate point and multi-point
openings are in place, and Gemini plus a compact BaseFold-style prototype
exist (one root, sumcheck plus Möbius FRI folds, optional Merkle caps).
The prototype and committed lookup are experimental and not wired into
AIR/STARK; batched openings, a hiding PCS/sumcheck, self-proving
recursion, and a zkVM remain roadmap items. No external audit has yet
been performed, for which the suites, gates, versioned wire format, and
pinned baselines are the evidence package.
